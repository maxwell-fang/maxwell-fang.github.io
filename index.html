About Me

I obtained my Bachelor's of Mathematics with a Major in Combinatorics and Optimization from the University of Waterloo where I worked with Stephen A. Vavasis from 2024-2026. My research interests are continuous optimization, computational linear algebra, and unsupervised/supervised learning; not necessarily applied to one another. During my time at the University of Waterloo, I also worked as a ML Engineer/Software Developer Co-op at CAA South Central Ontario, Eon Media, and Ford Motor Company developing mainly computer vision based products/tools.

An easily analyzable and scalable model for a dataset of greyscale images adhering to neural scaling laws in the spectra of the covariance matrix. This report is unpublished and was completed under the supervision of Stephen A. Vavasis.
